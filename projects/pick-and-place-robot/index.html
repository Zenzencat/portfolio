Autonomous Pick-and-Place Robot

Vision-guided 4-DOF robot arm with custom inverse kinematics

Team project · my role: robot architect (vision and arm integration), custom inverse-kinematics implementation, field tuning under competition conditions.

  • Intel RealSense
  • YOLOv8
  • OpenCV
  • Inverse kinematics
  • Arduino

What it is

A fully autonomous pick-and-place robot: Intel RealSense depth sensing, YOLOv8 + OpenCV object detection, and a 4-DOF arm driven by a custom inverse-kinematics pipeline — vision to actuation, end to end.

Why

The task was logistics-style pick-and-place: picking up objects and placing or stacking them in the right positions, which is exactly what real warehouse robots have to do.

How it works

  1. Integrate YOLOv8 + OpenCV detection with RealSense depth
  2. Custom IK pipeline for the 4-DOF arm
  3. Arduino servo control
  4. Field testing & competition runs
