Project Pelagic

Oil-slick detection in Sentinel-1 SAR satellite imagery with a U-Net

Team project · my role: lead developer (model, backend and front-end).

  • PyTorch
  • U-Net
  • FastAPI
  • SQLite
  • React
  • Leaflet.js

What it is

A maritime oil-slick detection system: U-Net segmentation over Sentinel-1 SAR satellite imagery, served through a full web pipeline so a spill can be seen on a map, not just in a tensor. A live pipeline fetches new Sentinel-1 scenes on demand from the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem and runs them through the same model, with nearby vessels matched from Global Fishing Watch AIS data.

Why

Oil slicks at sea are hard to spot early: optical satellites are blocked by cloud and darkness, while radar (SAR) sees through both — but SAR imagery needs trained models to separate slicks from look-alikes. Early detection shrinks response time and environmental damage.

How it works

  1. Assemble public Zenodo Sentinel-1 datasets (Parts I–III)
  2. Scene-aware train/test splitting to prevent leakage
  3. Train U-Net with BCE-Dice loss
  4. Build FastAPI inference service + SQLite
  5. React/Leaflet map front-end
  6. Live fetch: Sentinel-1 from Copernicus (CDSE) → inference → map, with AIS vessel matching

Results

Mean oil IoU of 0.724 on 10 held-out oil scenes (Zenodo Part III). Inspired by the MV PERKASA case (SkyTruth, 2019).

Limitations: Detection only by design.
