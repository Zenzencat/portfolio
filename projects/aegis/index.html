Project Aegis

Concept: offline-first scam protection for migrant workers

Concept designed entirely by me.

  • On-device AI
  • Offline-first design
  • Low-literacy UX

What it is

A concept for an offline-first scam-protection system for migrant workers: on-device AI designed to flag scam patterns without needing connectivity, data plans, or high digital literacy. This is a design concept, not a released app.

Why

Migrant workers are prime targets for call-centre scams and the least protected: language barriers, low digital literacy, and unreliable connectivity make cloud-based protection tools unusable for exactly the people who need them most.

How it works

  1. Study the target user's daily constraints
  2. Design offline-first architecture
  3. Design on-device scam-pattern detection
  4. Plan behavioural-anomaly signals beyond static blocklists
  5. Design low-literacy UX
  6. Pitch video & national-round presentation
